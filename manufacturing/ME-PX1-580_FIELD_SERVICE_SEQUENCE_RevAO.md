# PX-1 Rev.AO — Field service sequence

Routine drivetrain service must be possible without opening the sealed electronics volume.

## Rear side service order
1. Power off and mechanically unload tether.
2. Remove side transmission cover.
3. Remove external wheel retainer.
4. Remove rear wheel.
5. Remove compound z30/z40 gear.
6. Remove external labyrinth/grease protector.
7. Inspect shaft journal and seal area.
8. Withdraw rear shaft only if the internal AS5600 magnet/holder has been released or has enough axial clearance.
9. Remove rear carrier only when bearing/seal replacement is required.

## Front side service order
1. Remove side cover.
2. Remove wheel retainer.
3. Remove front wheel.
4. Remove z40 wheel gear.
5. Inspect stationary axle and bearing/bushing.

## Field-design rules
- No routine wheel/gear service may require opening the pressure body.
- Side cover fasteners must all be reachable with one common tool size.
- Wheel retention must use positive mechanical locking.
- Seal lip must never pass over a keyway, thread, burr, or sharp shoulder during assembly.
- FKM seal is preferred for final prototype testing.
- Spare kit should contain: 2 seals 10x22x7, 2 bearings 6000-2RS, external retainers, M4/M5 fasteners, grease, one z40 idler, one wheel bearing/bushing set.

## Prototype acceptance target
A trained technician should be able to replace one external wheel/gear set with ordinary hand tools without disturbing electronics, camera wiring, or the opposite-side drivetrain.
