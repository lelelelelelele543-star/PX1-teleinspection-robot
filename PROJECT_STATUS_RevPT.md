## Outcome

Rev.PT replaces the non-purchasable source-only Z16/Z40 bevel definition with a strength-screened catalog KHK Z18/Z45 pair while preserving the required 2.5:1 ratio and the verified rear-wheel X250 drive topology.

The selected m1.5 H pair fits only after a controlled local belly correction. The executed CadQuery model retains DN150 clearance and leaves a 5.5 mm nominal pressure floor below the gear pocket.

## Procurement correction

The tether connector candidate changes from the 12-pin/5 A SP21 version to the 7-pin/15 A family:

- cable side: WEIPU SP2110/S7II-1N, female sockets;
- crawler panel: WEIPU SP2112/P7-1N, male pins;
- 15 A, 500 V, IP68 family rating;
- seventh contact carries cable screen/drain; six insulated conductors remain unchanged.

Female contacts remain on the potentially energised tether end. A separate load-bearing cable termination keeps tensile load off the connector.

This is still a prototype candidate: IP68 and 500 V catalog markings do not replace submerged pressure, insulation, creepage, wet-mating prohibition and pull tests.
