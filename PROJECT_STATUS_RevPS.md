# PX-1 PROJECT STATUS — Rev.PS

Date: 2026-09-02  
Status: ACTIVE MARKETPLACE REDESIGN / PROTOTYPE GATE

## Outcome

Rev.PS translates the verified Proteus service architecture and the extracted MS5 help material into an obtainable-parts prototype plan. Rev.PR remains the validated body/packaging geometry underneath this revision; Rev.PS adds commercial interfaces and correct service details without claiming a machining release.

## Active mechanical architecture

- six Ø90 wheels, three per side;
- five m1 Z50 side gears at X50/X100/X150/X200/X250;
- rear-wheel long axle at X250 is the driven input;
- two 24 V gearmotors total, one per side;
- supported 2.5:1 bevel handoff; Rev.PT selects the purchasable m1.5 Z18/Z45 H pair while the source Z16/Z40 remains reference evidence;
- identical serviceable bearing/seal carrier at all six wheel stations;
- one-tool keyed quick-change wheel retained by a central M8 interface and protected cap;
- external manual parallelogram lift with standard clamping lever, Belleville stack and 150 N gas spring;
- separate serviceable two-axis camera;
- single strain-relieved sealed tether tail;
- no cartridges or cassettes.

## Evidence added

`reference/Proteus-CRP-150/flash/MS5_ANALYSIS.md` records the flash-image filesystem and the mechanical conclusions from factory help videos. Firmware is treated only as evidence of interfaces; PX-1 does not reuse it.

## Procurement control

`bom/REV_PS_MARKETPLACE_MECHANICAL_REDESIGN.md` separates:

1. genuine catalog/marketplace parts;
2. parts that require one physical sample and measurement;
3. custom pressure/geometry parts that must remain machined;
4. safety-critical HOLD items.

Marketplace presence is not equivalent to engineering release. Seller changes, substitute internals and incomplete drawings make sample measurement compulsory for motors, gears, gas springs, connectors and slip rings.
