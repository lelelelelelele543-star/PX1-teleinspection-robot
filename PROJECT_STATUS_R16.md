## Verification summary

`build_r16/validation.json` is generated by
`mechanical/cadquery/PX1_R16_ProteusRebuild.py` and checks solid validity,
ID150 hard-part containment, six wheel-ground contacts, low camera fit, low/high
link lengths, selected lift collisions, six hand-service retainers and the
non-overlapping body electronics reserve layout.

The report keeps wheel/shaft/hub pipe intersections as an advisory because the
tread is the compliant pipe-contact surface.  No R16 result certifies pressure,
traction, IP rating, gear tooth life, strength, supplier availability or a
finished camera seal.
