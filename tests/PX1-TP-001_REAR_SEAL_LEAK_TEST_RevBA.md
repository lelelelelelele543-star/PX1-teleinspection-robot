# PX1-TP-001 — Rear shaft sealing first-article test — Rev.BA

Applies to PX1-DR-001 rear shaft + PX1-DR-002 rear carrier.
Status: mandatory before RELEASE.

## Safety
Use regulated low-pressure air only. Never connect an unregulated compressor directly to the prototype. Use a relief valve and a pressure gauge. Personnel must not stand in line with covers/plugs during pressure testing.

## Pre-inspection
- inspect Ø10 seal land: no axial scratches, dents or keyway/thread under lip;
- confirm 6000-2RS 10x26x8 rotates smoothly;
- confirm FKM radial seal 10x22x7 and FKM O-ring 40x2 are undamaged;
- clean groove and flange faces;
- lightly lubricate seal lip and O-ring with compatible grease;
- use an assembly sleeve/tape so the seal lip never crosses an exposed thread/key edge.

## Dry pressure-decay test
1. Assemble one complete rear sealing module into a rigid test fixture representing the body wall.
2. Fit regulated air source, gauge and relief valve.
3. Raise internal gauge pressure slowly to 0.20 bar; isolate source; stabilize 5 min.
4. Hold 10 min and record pressure at start/end.
5. Repeat at 0.30 bar.
6. Prototype proof step: 0.50 bar maximum only after 0.20/0.30 bar tests pass and the fixture/body wall is mechanically verified for that pressure.
7. During each stage rotate shaft manually at least 20 revolutions in both directions, then repeat pressure observation.

## External leak localization
Apply leak-detection solution only to external static joints and carrier/body interface. Bubbles are a FAIL. Do not contaminate the dynamic seal lip with detergent.

## Wet confirmation
After dry test passes, place the external side of the sealed module under water while the inside remains dry and observable. Use only the previously passed regulated test pressure. Rotate the shaft slowly. Any continuous bubble stream or internal moisture is FAIL.

## Acceptance
PASS requires:
- no visible bubbles at static joints;
- no internal moisture;
- no seal lip displacement/damage;
- shaft remains smooth without binding;
- pressure-decay result is repeatable after shaft rotation.

A numeric allowable pressure-drop limit is intentionally not frozen until test-fixture volume, gauge accuracy and temperature drift are measured. Record raw pressure, temperature, duration and fixture volume for the first three tests; then set the production limit from real data.

## Post-test
Disassemble first article, inspect seal lip track and shaft surface under magnification, photograph, record dimensions/condition, replace any damaged seal. Only after successful first article may PX1-DR-001 and PX1-DR-002 be considered for RELEASE.
