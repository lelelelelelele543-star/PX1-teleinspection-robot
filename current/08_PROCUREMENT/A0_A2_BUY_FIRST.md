# Procurement — buy first for A0–A2

This list is intentionally conservative. Do not buy a production quantity around HOLD geometry.

## Buy/prepare once exact article is already verified
- bearings/bushings matching released shaft diameters;
- inexpensive hardware for the drivetrain fixture;
- two traction driver channels for bench testing;
- MCU/controller + RS-485 modules for the bench;
- DC/DC and basic protection parts;
- current/voltage measurement means;
- six-core tether sample/available stock for electrical qualification.

## Buy only after interface lock
- traction motors: only after physical mount/shaft envelope is compatible with the 6×6 body and Z16 input;
- Z16/Z40 bevel gears: only as a matched pair with known module, angle, bore and mounting distance;
- production Z50 set: only after width/bore/material/backlash and side-cover clearance are locked;
- wheel set: production purchase only when exact DN150 wheel profile/interface is confirmed;
- final shaft seals/O-rings: only after groove/shaft finish and material requirements are frozen.

## Do NOT let these block A0
- final camera cosmetic housing;
- final reel frame;
- OSD polish;
- 100–150 m tether quantity;
- production case/labels.

Every actual purchase must be reflected in `../PX1_Current_BOM.csv` with article, source, quantity and release state.
