# 02 ELECTRICAL

Scope: 24 V input, DC/DC rails, traction drivers, MCU, RS-485, pressure sensing, lighting supply/control, camera power/video interfaces, protection and connectors.

Prototype principle: ready-made serviceable modules, no custom main PCB required.

Immediate work:
1. bench power tree;
2. two traction channels under real motor load;
3. RS-485 command link;
4. emergency/communication-loss motor stop;
5. pressure input;
6. camera + lighting integration;
7. 40 m tether voltage-drop/EMC test.

Existing `../ELECTRICAL_Current.md` remains the detailed electrical reference until replaced by verified measurements.
