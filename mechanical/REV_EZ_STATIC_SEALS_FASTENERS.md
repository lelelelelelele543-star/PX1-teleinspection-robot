# PX-1 Rev.EZ — static seals, cover lands and fasteners

Status: prototype detail baseline.

## Pressure zones
- P0 central body;
- P1 left side drive;
- P2 right side drive.
Each zone remains independently pressure-tested and sensed.

## Side covers
Current nominal external plate: 276 x 81 x 5 mm Al 6082-T6.

Seal philosophy:
- one closed-loop FKM face O-ring around each side-drive cavity;
- three independent local flange O-rings, one around each wheel station;
- no screw penetrates a pressure cavity inside the seal line unless specifically sealed.

Do not use the previously misinterpreted `190 mm` as seal-path length. Any 190 designation from source material is treated only according to the actual O-ring standard/vendor drawing.

## Side-cover groove
Until exact molded O-ring is selected, use only a machining coupon target:
- section 2.5 mm class;
- squeeze 20–24%;
- groove fill <85%;
- sealing surface Ra <=1.6 um;
- no cutter marks crossing the O-ring path;
- internal groove corner radius compatible with the chosen ring.

Final groove width/depth are supplier-controlled after actual ring selection.

## Wheel-flange static seal
Each removable axle flange gets its own FKM O-ring, approximately 32 x 1.5 class pending supplier freeze.

Requirements:
- O-ring remains on a static face only;
- flange centered by pilot diameter, not M4 screw clearance;
- minimum continuous sealing land >=3 mm outside groove where packaging allows;
- 3 x M4 fasteners on ~40 mm PCD candidate;
- anti-galling compound on stainless/aluminum interface.

## Top service cover
Top P0 cover uses a separate closed-loop FKM face seal.

Candidate plate:
- 5 mm Al 6082;
- dowel located;
- screw line outside seal groove;
- fastener pitch 35–45 mm class;
- M4 A4-80 or M5 where local structure permits.

The lift pivots and tether recovery loads must not be reacted through the top cover.

## Fastener policy
- external wet-zone screws: A4 stainless;
- Torx or hex socket preferred;
- captive/retained hardware where possible;
- repeated-service holes in aluminum receive steel thread inserts if cycle testing shows wear;
- no countersink through a thin sealing land;
- lower side-cover screw heads must remain inside DN150 clearance envelope.

## Cover location
Use two dowel pins per major cover plus machined pilot/step where possible. Bolts clamp; pilots locate.

## Prototype qualification
For each sealed joint:
1. dry assembly and torque log;
2. +0.25 bar leak test;
3. 60 min pressure/temperature decay log;
4. submerged static test;
5. 20 open/close cycles;
6. repeat leak test;
7. side bays additionally tested with all three shafts rotating.

Structural proof is separated from routine operating pressure. Normal positive pressure remains approximately +0.20…+0.30 bar gauge.
