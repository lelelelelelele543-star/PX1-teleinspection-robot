# PX-1 Rear Shaft Stack — Rev.AN

Rev.AN corrects the previous 54 mm rear-shaft envelope. That length did not leave enough room for a serviceable stack containing the bearing, spacer, seal, external compound gear, 18 mm wheel hub and positive external retention.

## New prototype shaft length
- Shaft diameter: 10 mm
- Overall length: 62 mm
- Bearing journal: Ø10 h6
- Seal journal: Ø10 h6, Ra <= 0.4
- Magnet pocket: Ø6.1 x 3.2 mm on dry-side end
- External retention zone: M8 thread, 10 mm long
- External torque transfer: 3 x 3 mm keyway, limited to the gear/wheel zone

## Axial stack, dry side -> water side
| Zone | Position, mm | Item |
|---|---:|---|
| magnet | 0...3.2 | diametric AS5600 magnet |
| bearing | 4...12 | 6000-2RS, 10x26x8 |
| spacer | 12...15 | removable spacer |
| radial seal | 15...22 | 10x22x7 FKM preferred |
| labyrinth/grease | 22...25 | sacrificial contamination barrier |
| compound gear | 25...33 | m1 z30/z40 |
| wheel hub | 33...51 | 18 mm wheel |
| retention | 52...62 | M8 external thread / all-metal locknut |

## Mandatory design rules
1. No thread, keyway, cross-hole or D-flat may pass under the seal lip.
2. The wheel and external gear must be removable without opening the pressure body.
3. The shaft must be replaceable from the outside after removal of the rear carrier.
4. Bearing and seal seats remain coaxial within the tolerances already defined for the prototype carrier.
5. Final M8 thread, keyway end shape and locknut hardware are validated on the first physical shaft before serial release.
