# PX-1 Rev.FX — Group A manufacturing drawing candidates

Status: DRAWING CANDIDATE only. These dimensions are derived from the currently validated PX-1 solids and are not serial machining release.

## DRW-PX1-431 — Wheel shaft
Material candidate: 40X / 42CrMo4 with corrosion protection, or precipitation-hardening stainless after supplier/process review.

Axial stack from inboard datum A:
1. Ø12 h6 x 23.0 — gear / inner support region.
2. Ø17 h6 x 7.0 — 61903 journal.
3. Ø19 seal land x 4.0 — dynamic X-ring running surface, final tolerance follows exact seal supplier.
4. Ø21 x 2.0 — labyrinth collar.
5. Ø17 h6 x 18.0 — wheel seat.
Total length 54.0 mm.

Features:
- wheel keyway 4x4x12, positioned entirely outboard of the seal land;
- M6 axial retention at wheel end;
- seal land Ra <=0.4 um, polished, no spiral tool marks;
- bearing journals Ra <=0.8 um target;
- runout seal land to bearing journal <=0.02 mm CTQ;
- transition into seal land polished/chamfered with no sharp edge.

## DRW-PX1-433 — Side-drive cover
Material: EN AW-6082 T6.

Nominal:
- 286 x 86 x 5 mm;
- 3 x Ø36 axle-flange pilot openings at X=50 / 150 / 250, common Z=45 in crawler datum system;
- 12 x Ø3.4 M3 clearance holes at current solid coordinates;
- lower fastener heads must be flush/recessed because DN150 clearance is the limiting geometry.

Main static seal candidate:
- source-size O-ring: 190 x 1.5;
- equal-length racetrack centerline about 261.920 x 64.000;
- preliminary groove 1.90 wide x 1.20 deep;
- nominal face squeeze 20%;
- nominal groove fill 77.5%.

The groove dimensions remain supplier/qualification controlled. Do not release production machining until the actual FKM ring is selected and coupon-tested.

CTQ:
- sealing face flatness <=0.05 mm over whole seal path;
- Ra <=1.6 um;
- no cutter marks crossing seal path;
- axle pilot positions from master datum +/-0.03 mm target.
