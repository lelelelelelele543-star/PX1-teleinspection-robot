# PX-1 Rev.AQ — wheel / gear / cover service interface

## Design intent
The external drivetrain must remain field-serviceable without opening the dry pressure body.

## Wheel/hub prototype
- shaft/hub bore: 10 mm
- key: 3x3 mm prototype
- hub OD: 28 mm
- hub length: 18 mm
- external retention target: M8 nut + washer
- socket access target: 13 mm, subject to exact selected fastener standard

The shaft keyway must not extend into the rotary-seal running surface.

## Side cover prototype
- nominal thickness: 3 mm
- six M4 perimeter fasteners
- M4 captive fasteners preferred so screws are not lost in a pipe/worksite
- cover removal target: possible without removing wheels
- service openings must not expose the pressure-body seal directly to debris

## Release holds
These dimensions are not yet machining-release dimensions. Before `/release`:
1. select exact M8 nut/washer standard;
2. select wheel material and tire-to-hub attachment method;
3. check socket envelope in full FreeCAD assembly;
4. check cover-to-wheel and cover-to-gear clearances over tolerance stack;
5. decide aluminium vs polymer cover after impact/debris test;
6. add captive or retained M4 cover hardware.

## Field-tool target
One compact kit: 3/4/5 mm hex keys, 8/10/13 mm sockets, small flat screwdriver/pick, grease and seal tools. Final tool list follows selected purchased fasteners.
