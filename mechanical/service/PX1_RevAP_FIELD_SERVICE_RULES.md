# PX-1 Rev.AP — field service rules

Goal: the crawler must be maintainable with ordinary hand tools without opening the pressure body for routine drivetrain service.

## Level 1 — field service, sealed body stays closed
1. Remove side gear cover.
2. Remove wheel-retention hardware.
3. Remove wheel.
4. Remove accessible z40 gear / idler gear as required.
5. Inspect grease, tooth damage, axle play and debris.
6. Reassemble and verify free rotation.

No electronics, rear bearing or shaft seal should be disturbed during Level 1 work.

## Level 2 — workshop drivetrain service
1. Complete Level 1 strip-down.
2. Remove external rear gear hardware.
3. Withdraw rear shaft only when bearing/seal service is actually required.
4. Replace 6000-2RS and/or 10x22x7 seal.
5. Inspect Ø10 h6 seal track. Reject shaft if the seal track has scoring, corrosion or a groove under the lip.
6. Reassemble with fresh lubricant and perform leak/pressure test before operation.

## Required ordinary tools
- metric hex keys;
- 8/10/13 mm spanners/sockets as applicable to final fasteners;
- small circlip pliers if circlips are retained in final revision;
- small torque wrench for controlled reassembly;
- plastic/nylon drift;
- no dedicated bearing puller for Level 1 service.

## Design acceptance criteria
- each side cover must be removable independently;
- wheel-retention hardware must be visible and reachable directly;
- no service fastener may be hidden behind the pressure-body cover;
- routine gear service must not unload or rotate the seal carrier;
- rear bearing/seal replacement is intentionally a second-level operation;
- left and right service procedure must be symmetric;
- fastener variety should be minimized to M4/M5 plus wheel-retention hardware.

## Current status
Rev.AP is a CAD/service-access validation revision. Tool clearances remain prototype-level until the exact wheel hub, exact z40 hardware and final cover geometry are physically verified.
