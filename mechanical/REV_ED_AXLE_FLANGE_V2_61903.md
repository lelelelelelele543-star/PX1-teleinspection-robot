# PX-1 Rev.ED — axle flange V2 with 61903 outer bearing

Status: preferred prototype wheel-flange architecture. Supersedes Rev.DZ outer-bearing stack.

## Source alignment
The uploaded CRP150 side-drive drawing DRW-002-374 uses, for three wheel axles, three 61903-2RS bearings (17x30x7), six 61801-2RS bearings (12x21x5), three axle flanges, three 32x1.5 O-rings and three X-rings. PX-1 adopts that load-path philosophy with its own geometry and FKM sealing choice.

## Stepped wheel shaft
Preferred PX-1 shaft family:
- inner gear/bearing journals: Ø12 h6;
- Z50 keyed seat: Ø12 with 4x4 parallel key;
- second inner bearing journal: Ø12 h6;
- transition shoulder to Ø17;
- outer wheel-load journal: Ø17 h6 for 61903-2RS;
- dynamic-seal journal adjacent to bearing: final Ø17–18 class after seal selection;
- wheel positive-drive section outboard;
- M6 internal axial retaining thread at end.

Material candidate: 40Cr13 / 1.4034 corrosion-resistant hardenable steel. Seal journal finish target Ra <=0.4 um, no keyway/thread/groove under the dynamic seal.

## Axle flange
Prototype envelope:
- outside diameter: Ø48–50 mm;
- material: Al 6082-T6 hard anodized or stainless if corrosion testing demands it;
- outer bearing counterbore: Ø30 H7 x 7 mm minimum plus assembly relief;
- static flange seal: FKM O-ring in the 32x1.5 nominal class, exact groove from supplier standard;
- locating pilot: approximately Ø38 class, sized around the Ø30 bearing pocket and O-ring land;
- 3x M4 fasteners on PCD40 remains the PX-1 service candidate if enough metal remains around the O-ring groove.

The bearing seat, static O-ring groove and fastener holes must be dimensioned concentrically from the wheel axis datum.

## Dynamic seal choice
Do not blindly copy the original X-ring material/size.

Two prototype candidates must be compared:
A. FKM quad/X-ring on a polished Ø17/18-class journal;
B. compact FKM rotary lip seal if available within the Ø30 bearing/flange envelope.

Selection criteria:
- submerged leakage;
- starting torque/drag;
- grit tolerance;
- wear after 5000+ revolutions;
- low-temperature elasticity;
- replaceability with ordinary tools.

Positive pressure +0.20…+0.30 bar assists leak detection but is not used as the primary seal.

## Bearing load path
Wheel radial loads enter:
`wheel -> Ø17 shaft section -> 61903 outer bearing -> axle flange/body`

Gear alignment is supported separately by the pair of 61801 bearings on the Ø12 inner section. This prevents the small inner bearings from carrying the full wheel bending moment.

## Axial retention
- inner bearings retained by shoulders/circlips as appropriate;
- Z50 retained between defined spacers/shoulders, not allowed to float into the cover;
- 61903 retained axially by flange shoulder + removable retainer or cover step;
- wheel retained by M6 end screw/disk; end screw carries axial retention only, not drive torque.

## Service sequence
1. isolate/depressurize affected side only;
2. remove wheel retaining disk and wheel;
3. remove 3 flange fasteners;
4. extract flange with 61903 + dynamic seal service components;
5. inspect Ø17/18 journal under magnification;
6. renew dynamic seal and static O-ring;
7. reassemble and torque fasteners evenly;
8. fill only affected P1/P2 zone and leak-test before powered rotation.

## Release gates
- actual 61903 and 61801 samples measured;
- FKM quad-ring/lip-seal selected and tested;
- flange wall thickness checked after real groove geometry;
- DN150 sweep includes flange, M4 heads and wheel recess;
- 1 bar differential flange coupon test;
- submerged rotation endurance passes before production drawing release.
