# PX-1 Rev.BV — internal ROLL cartridge

Status: packaging/drawing-candidate concept, not machining release.

## Architecture
The Ø52 mm camera-head outer shell remains stationary and sealed. Continuous ROLL is achieved by a smaller internal cartridge rotating inside the dry head volume.

## Current cartridge candidate
- cartridge OD: 30 mm
- cartridge length: 46 mm
- central through-bore: 17 mm
- support: 2x 6803-2RS, 17x26x5 mm
- driven gear: m0.5 z51, pitch Ø25.5 mm, outside Ø~26.5 mm, face 3 mm candidate
- central rotary-transfer keep-out: Ø12.5 x 24 mm prototype reservation
- camera mount plate: 22x22x2.5 mm envelope for 19x19 camera

## Clearance
With a 52 mm outer head and nominal 2.5 mm shell wall, inner shell diameter is approximately 47 mm before local ribs/features. A 30 mm cartridge therefore leaves substantial radial packaging space for the ROLL motor, pinion, wiring and TILT interfaces.

Target clearances:
- rotating cartridge to fixed shell/ribs: >=1.5 mm nominal
- non-rotating internal parts: >=0.5 mm nominal
- axial cartridge freedom after retention: 0.2–0.5 mm target

## Important boundary rule
The 6803 bearings are mechanical supports only. They are not credited as pressure/water seals. The pressure boundary stays on the fixed outer shell, front optical window and rear service interface.

## Service concept
The internal cartridge should be removable rearward after the camera-head quick-release/service module is opened. Routine replacement of camera/ROLL cartridge must not require destructive seal removal.

## Remaining holds
1. exact 75-ohm CVBS rotary transfer;
2. exact bearing housing fit after aluminium/POM cartridge material is selected;
3. gear retention and backlash adjustment method;
4. camera mounting screws/plate geometry;
5. internal wire routing through TILT movement;
6. final mass/inertia and ROLL motor-current test.

## Result
At envelope level, the internal-cartridge architecture comfortably fits the Ø52 mm head target and is preferred over a rotating external pressure shell.
