Interpretation
--------------
The overlapped coupling-hub / 6704 support concept is geometrically plausible inside the current 12 mm rear side-bay envelope and its first-order key/shaft loads are modest at the existing 4 N.m design screen.

This does NOT release the design. The 0.35 mm axial packaging remainder is small and must absorb real bearing width tolerance, shoulder/chamfer requirements, gear axial retention and assembly clearance. A full solid model is mandatory.
