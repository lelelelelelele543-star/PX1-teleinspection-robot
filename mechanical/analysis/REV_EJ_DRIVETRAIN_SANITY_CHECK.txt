PX-1 Rev.EJ drivetrain / pressure sanity check

Inputs used for prototype sizing:
- motor-side pinion torque limit: 1.50 N*m
- bevel ratio: 2.5:1
- assumed bevel efficiency for sanity check: 0.90
- resulting side-transfer torque: 3.375 N*m
- wheel gear: m1 Z50, pitch radius 25 mm
- wheel OD: 90 mm
- motor candidate speed: 107 rpm
- side cover: 276 x 81 mm
- top service cover: 275 x 74 mm

TORSION, SOLID ROUND SHAFT NOMINAL:
- Ø8 pinion shaft @1.50 N*m: 14.92 MPa
- Ø10 transfer shaft @3.375 N*m: 17.19 MPa
- Ø12 wheel/gear shaft @3.375 N*m: 9.95 MPa
- Ø17 outer wheel journal @3.375 N*m: 3.50 MPa

GEAR FORCE SANITY:
- Z50 pitch-line tangential force @3.375 N*m: 135 N
- Z18 m1.5 pinion pitch-line tangential force @1.50 N*m: ~111 N

DOG COUPLING SANITY CANDIDATE:
Assume mean torque radius 8.5 mm, two opposed dogs, each face 4 x 6 mm.
- total tangential force: ~397 N
- load per dog: ~199 N
- average face pressure: ~8.27 MPa
Conclusion: mechanically comfortable for steel coupling candidate; wear/fretting/endurance remain test gates.

SPEED:
- motor 107 rpm / 2.5 = 42.8 wheel rpm ideal
- Ø90 circumference = 0.282743 m
- ideal no-load crawler speed = ~12.10 m/min
Real loaded/inspection speed will be lower and controlled electronically.

PRESSURE COVER LOADS:
At +0.30 bar gauge:
- one 276x81 side-cover projected load: ~671 N
- 275x74 top-cover projected load: ~611 N
At 1.0 bar differential qualification target:
- side cover: ~2236 N total
- top cover: ~2035 N total
These totals are low relative to clamp capacity of perimeter M4 hardware; cover plate stiffness/flatness and seal land behavior remain the governing checks.

GEOMETRIC FIXED RELATIONSHIPS:
- equal m1 Z50 gears -> nominal center distance = 50.00 mm
- five-gear side train positions X=50/100/150/200/250 -> wheel pitch = 100.00 mm
- middle station X=150 aligns side-drive input with central bevel transfer.

DESIGN CONCLUSION:
Current prototype drivetrain is not limited by nominal shaft torsional stress. Priority risks remain:
1. actual JGB37 stall/current/gearbox behavior;
2. KHK bevel contact and hub connection;
3. transfer dog-coupling wear/repeatability;
4. side-drive bearing alignment after cover service;
5. dynamic-seal friction and leakage;
6. DN150 collision with real screws/flanges/lift/tether;
7. thermal behavior of motors/drivers/DC-DC.

No machining release from this calculation alone.
