## Traction gears

- 10 x m1 Z50 spur gears, 5 each side.
- 2 x bevel pinions, m1.5 Z18 H-class candidate.

The KHK family remains the preferred dimensional reference, but exact purchased articles remain a physical procurement gate.

- 6 x 61903-class outer wheel-load bearings, one per wheel.
- 2 x 61800-class bearings for the two X200 bevel-output shafts.
- 2 x 61801-class bearings for the two supported bevel-pinion shafts.
