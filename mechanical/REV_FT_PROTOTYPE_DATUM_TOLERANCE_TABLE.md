# PX-1 Rev.FT — prototype manufacturing datums and tolerance table

Status: DRAWING-CANDIDATE only. These values organize the first prototype drawings; they are not a serial machining release.

## Datum system
Main body:
- Datum A: machined lower floor plane of P0.
- Datum B: longitudinal center plane Y=0.
- Datum C: rear structural bulkhead face.
- Wheel-station X coordinates are dimensioned from C through the master body coordinate system, not chained station-to-station.

Side drive:
- Datum S1: side-cover sealing face.
- Datum S2: axle-flange pilot diameter.
- Datum S3: wheel-axis center plane Z=45.

Camera/lift:
- Datum L1: lower body pivot axis.
- Datum L2: body center plane Y=0.
- Datum L3: camera tilt axis.

## Body target tolerances
General machined dimensions unless otherwise stated: ISO 2768-m class candidate.
Critical exceptions:
- wheel-axis X positions relative to master datum: ±0.03 mm target;
- wheel-axis Z positions: ±0.03 mm target;
- left/right corresponding axle-axis coaxiality where applicable: 0.05 mm target;
- X200 output bearing/seal bore coaxiality: ≤0.03 mm;
- side-cover sealing faces flatness: 0.05 mm over full cover land;
- top-cover sealing face flatness: 0.05 mm;
- static sealing surface finish: Ra ≤1.6 µm;
- dynamic lip/X-ring shaft land: Ra ≤0.4 µm, no spiral tool marks.

## Wheel shaft
Prototype candidate:
- inner gear/support journal Ø12 h6;
- outer 61903 journal Ø17 h6;
- dynamic X-ring running land Ø19 with final tolerance set from selected seal supplier;
- wheel seat Ø17 h6 candidate;
- M6 axial retainer;
- 4x4 keyway outside the seal land.

Critical:
- runout of seal land to bearing journal ≤0.02 mm target;
- no keyway, thread, circlip groove or cross-hole under seal contact path;
- seal-entry edge polished/chamfered.

## Axle flange
- outer pilot geometry locates flange; M3 screws clamp only;
- 61903 pocket Ø30 H7 candidate;
- flange/body pilot fit H7/g6-class candidate after actual machining process is chosen;
- flange sealing face Ra ≤1.6 µm;
- 4 x M3 on PCD40 candidate;
- external projection limited to 3 mm by DN150 packaging.

## X200 bevel output
- KHK large bevel seat Ø10 h6 candidate;
- 61800 journal Ø10 h6;
- seal land Ø18 h9 candidate, Ra ≤0.4 µm;
- service coupling seat Ø12 h6;
- bearing and seal bores machined in one setup where possible;
- bevel mounting distance is not frozen from nominal CAD: final shim pack set after real gear contact-pattern test.

## Lift arms
- plate thickness 5.0 mm candidate;
- paired pivot bores Ø8 H7 candidate;
- pivot-center distance 120.00 ±0.05 mm target;
- upper/lower parallel links matched as pairs;
- camera-side pivot spacing 20.00 ±0.05 mm target;
- no permanent welded pivots.

## Camera shell
- target OD Ø52 mm;
- concentricity of front window seat, internal ROLL bearing seats and rear closure pilot ≤0.05 mm target;
- window sealing face Ra ≤0.8–1.6 µm depending selected O-ring;
- optical-retainer face perpendicular to optical axis ≤0.05 mm over diameter target.

## Fasteners
Wet-zone service fasteners:
- A4 stainless candidate;
- Torx/hex socket;
- lower side-cover heads flush or recessed;
- reusable aluminum threads get inserts where service-cycle test shows wear.

## Release rule
Any tolerance tied to a purchased bearing, seal, gear, connector, camera board or gas spring is superseded by the exact manufacturer's current drawing once that part is physically selected.
