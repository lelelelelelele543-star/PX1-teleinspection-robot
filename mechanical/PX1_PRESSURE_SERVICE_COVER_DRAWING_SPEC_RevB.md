# PX-1 pressure/camera service cover — drawing specification Rev.B screen

Drawing status: **FOR PROTOTYPE LAYOUT ONLY — NOT FOR MACHINING RELEASE**

## Cover plate

- material target: EN AW-6082-T6;
- nominal blank: 78 x 42 x 6 mm;
- datum A: sealing face;
- datum B: long side;
- datum C: cover centreline;
- corner radii: R3 screen, may change with final roof geometry;
- external edges deburr 0.2–0.5 mm; do not round away the sealing land.

## Opening below cover

Body-opening screen:

- 58 x 24 mm;
- centred under cover;
- nominal seal land remaining: 10 mm in X and 9 mm in Y.

Do not machine the body opening from this note until full-master integration is checked.

## Fasteners

Screen:

- 2 x M4;
- X pitch 66 mm;
- centres X224 and X290 in crawler coordinates;
- Y0;
- blind body threads preferred so service does not create a second through-pressure path;
- captive/retained screws preferred for field service.

Final hole, counterbore/countersink, thread engagement and torque depend on selected screw head and boss thickness.

## Face seal

Screen only:

- target cord Ø2.0 mm;
- provisional groove width 2.5 mm;
- provisional groove depth 1.5 mm;
- continuous closed groove with generous corner radii;
- no groove break at screws, fill valve or gland.

Final groove is recalculated from purchased seal diameter, hardness, target compression, groove fill and thermal expansion.

## Fill port

Reserve a local boss around X268 / Y0 for:

- M10x1 or G1/8 class low-profile fill valve;
- exposed hard envelope <=Ø14 x 6 mm above cover;
- cap/removal tool access.

Do not drill/thread before exact valve is selected.

## Cable gland

LAPP 53112000 is packaged horizontally in a body/cover-side boss, not standing vertically on the cover.

Reserve:

- M12 x 1.5 thread;
- >=6.5 mm thread engagement geometry for sample check;
- tool access for 16 mm wrench class;
- external envelope Ø17.6 x 26.5 mm class;
- cable exit clear of sharp edges and lift pivot.

## Marking

Laser engraving / durable marking target:

`PRESSURE / CAMERA SERVICE`

Optional second line after valve article is frozen:

`DEPRESSURIZE BEFORE OPENING`
