# PX-1 pressure/camera service cover — drawing specification Rev.B / WB23E screen

Drawing status: **FOR PROTOTYPE LAYOUT ONLY — NOT FOR MACHINING RELEASE**

WB23E supersedes the earlier WB23C/D `78 x 42` cover / `58 x 24` opening layout because the original M4-hole-to-seal-groove web was too small after a realistic face-seal groove was considered.

## Cover plate

- material target: EN AW-6082-T6;
- nominal blank: **86 x 44 x 6 mm**;
- crawler centre: **X261 / Y0**;
- X span: **218...304 mm**;
- datum A: sealing face;
- datum B: long side;
- datum C: cover centreline;
- corner radii: R3 screen, may change with final pressure-roof geometry;
- external edges deburr 0.2–0.5 mm; do not round away the sealing land.

## Opening below cover

Body-opening screen:

- **48 x 22 mm**;
- X range: **237...285 mm**;
- Y range: **-11...+11 mm**;
- centred on cover Y;
- dry JST VH 8-position connector family envelope (~31.62 x 10.5 mm) fits through this opening at screen level.

Do not machine the body opening from this note until real connector/gland/valve samples and the final pressure-body drawing are checked.

## Fasteners

Screen:

- **2 x M4**;
- M4 clearance-hole study diameter: **Ø4.5 mm**;
- screw-head hard-envelope study: **Ø8 mm**;
- X pitch: **70 mm**;
- centres: **X226 and X296**, Y0 in crawler coordinates;
- blind body threads preferred so service does not create another through-pressure path;
- retained/captive screws preferred for field service.

WB23E screen margins:

- minimum M4 clearance-hole edge to provisional seal-groove outer edge: **3.5 mm**;
- minimum Ø8 head-envelope edge to cover end: **4.0 mm**.

Final hole form, counterbore/countersink, screw length, thread engagement and torque depend on the purchased screw and body-boss thickness.

## Face seal

Screen only:

- target elastomer cord: Ø2.0 mm FKM class;
- provisional groove centre-path offset from service-opening edge: **4.0 mm**;
- provisional groove width: **2.5 mm**;
- provisional groove depth: **1.5 mm** only as a packaging study;
- continuous closed groove with generous corner radii;
- no groove break at screws, fill valve or gland;
- minimum provisional groove outer edge to cover Y edge: **5.75 mm**.

**Groove depth/width are not released machining dimensions.** Final values are recalculated from the actual purchased elastomer diameter, hardness, squeeze, gland fill, surface finish and thermal expansion.

## Fill / pressure port

Reserve a local boss around **X273 / Y0**.

Current hard packaging envelope in WB23E remains:

- no more than Ø14 mm;
- no more than 6 mm exposed height in the current screen;
- low-profile compressor-gun / Schrader-compatible or equivalent service interface;
- cap/removal-tool access.

A later pressure-port sensitivity study controls the preferred smaller envelope. Do not drill/thread before the exact valve is selected.

## Cable gland

LAPP `SKINTOP MS-M 53112000` is packaged horizontally in a pressure-pod front/side boss, not standing vertically on the cover.

Reserve:

- M12 x 1.5 thread;
- >=6.5 mm thread-engagement geometry for sample check;
- tool access for 16 mm wrench class;
- external envelope Ø17.6 x 26.5 mm class;
- nominal axis Y+12 / Z103;
- cable exit clear of sharp edges and lift pivot.

## Marking

Laser engraving / durable marking target:

`PRESSURE / CAMERA SERVICE`

Second line:

`DEPRESSURIZE BEFORE OPENING`

## Controlling status

WB23E controls prototype cover/opening/screw geometry. WB23C remains the functional service-topology definition and WB23D remains the first full-body integration proof.
