# PX-1 Rev.FZ — Group A prototype machining sequence

Status: process candidate for first prototype lot.

## 431 wheel shafts — qty 6 + 1 spare recommended
Preferred route:
1. Cut corrosion-resistant/alloy-steel bar oversize.
2. Face and center-drill both ends.
3. Rough-turn all diameters in one setup where possible.
4. Finish Ø12 and Ø17 bearing journals.
5. Finish Ø19 dynamic X-ring land last, leaving polishing allowance.
6. Turn Ø21 labyrinth collar and Ø17 wheel seat.
7. Machine wheel-end axial M6 retention.
8. Mill 4x4x12 keyway only in wheel-seat region.
9. Polish Ø19 seal land longitudinally to Ra <=0.4 um; no helical finish.
10. Deburr and inspect before any coating/passivation.

Do not allow blasting, aggressive polishing or coating buildup on bearing/seal journals without masking/process compensation.

## 432 axle flanges — qty 6 + 1 spare recommended
1. Turn Ø50 outer disk and inboard locating spigot from one setup.
2. Bore central shaft clearance.
3. Bore Ø30 61903 pocket from same spindle datum if machine allows.
4. Face sealing/locating surfaces.
5. Transfer to mill; drill 4 x M3 clearance on PCD40.
6. Add static seal groove only after final O-ring article is frozen.
7. Add dynamic X-ring gland only after supplier gland geometry is frozen.
8. Deburr without rounding pilot or bearing-seat edges.

## 433 side covers — qty 2
1. Start from stress-relieved 6082-T6 plate thicker than final 5 mm.
2. Face both sides to final thickness/flatness.
3. Establish cover datum edges.
4. Machine three Ø36 pilot openings in one coordinate setup.
5. Drill 12 M3 clearance holes.
6. Machine main seal groove in same setup as pilot pattern where possible.
7. Machine lower fastener-head recesses flush with DN150 envelope.
8. Final light face pass on sealing surface if required.
9. Deburr groove edges by controlled hand stone, not rotary abrasive crossing the seal path.

## 434 X200 output shafts — qty 2 + 1 spare recommended
1. Turn from alloy/stainless steel bar.
2. Keep Ø10 gear seat, Ø10 61800 journal and Ø18 seal land on one turning axis.
3. Finish/polish Ø18 seal land after all other cutting operations.
4. Machine Ø12 service coupling seat and D-flat only outboard of seal land.
5. Drill/tap retention feature at bevel end as finally selected.
6. Inspect runout before coating/passivation.

## 435 X200 bearing/seal bosses — qty 2
1. Turn outside Ø38 and both axial faces.
2. Bore Ø19 61800 seat and Ø30 seal pocket in one chucking/setup if possible.
3. Bore through clearance.
4. Preserve coaxiality <=0.03 mm target.
5. Trial-fit actual bearing and seal before mating-body bore is frozen.

## 436 bevel pinion shafts — qty 2 + 1 spare recommended
1. Turn Ø6 / Ø8 / Ø12 steps from one centerline.
2. Finish Ø12 61801 seat.
3. Machine final retaining end after actual bearing + KHK gear stack is measured.
4. Trial-fit motor coupling, gear and bearing.

## Prototype lot strategy
Do not machine the whole vehicle in one batch before learning from the first side.
Recommended order:
- one 431 + one 432 + one 435 + one 434 + one 436;
- bench fit and measure;
- correct only if evidence requires it;
- then produce remaining symmetric parts.

This reduces the chance of repeating an unverified seal/bearing fit across all six wheel stations.
