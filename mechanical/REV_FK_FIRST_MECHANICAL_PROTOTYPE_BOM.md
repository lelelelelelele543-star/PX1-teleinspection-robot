## Prototype order strategy
Do not buy six/several expensive machined copies before first-article inspection.
Recommended order:
1. machine 1 wheel shaft + 1 flange + seal coupon;
2. buy 1 each of 61801, 61903, X-ring and 32x1.5 O-ring for fit test;
3. pressure/rotation bench-test the single station;
4. only then machine the remaining five wheel stations;
5. separately validate one KHK bevel pair and one motor before ordering the complete traction set.
