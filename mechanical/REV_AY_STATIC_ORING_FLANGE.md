# PX-1 Rev.AY — static flange O-ring freeze

Status: DRAWING-CANDIDATE.

## Selected seal
- O-ring: 40x2 mm
- material: FKM
- preferred hardness: 75–80 Shore A
- preferred reference: Dichtomatik 67013345 / equivalent 40x2 FKM

## Seal type
Static axial face seal between rear carrier flange and crawler body.
Internal crawler pressure acts outward, so groove outside diameter is the controlling datum.

## Groove geometry — prototype target
- groove outside diameter: 44.0 mm
- groove width: 2.50 mm radial
- groove inside diameter: 39.0 mm
- groove depth: 1.50 mm
- nominal O-ring squeeze: 25%
- nominal gland fill: about 84%
- groove corner radius: 0.15–0.20 mm max target
- flange sealing-face finish: Ra <=1.6 µm target

## Carrier flange / bolt pattern revision
Previous Ø56 flange / PCD48 is superseded because it leaves insufficient material between the O-ring groove and M4 holes.
New target:
- flange OD: 60 mm
- bolt pattern: 4x Ø4.5 on PCD54
- minimum nominal radial ligament between groove OD and M4 hole edge: about 2.75 mm

## Assembly
- lightly lubricate O-ring with compatible silicone-free/FKM-compatible assembly lubricant;
- do not use sharp tools to seat ring;
- mating face must be free of radial scratches, dents and burrs;
- tighten four M4 fasteners in diagonal sequence;
- final torque to be frozen after selected screw grade, thread engagement and body material are fixed.

## Release gate
Carrier flange may move to RELEASE only after:
1. actual 40x2 FKM O-ring is fitted;
2. compression is verified with feeler/measurement or witness method;
3. 0.3 bar internal pressure leak test passes for 30 min;
4. repeat assembly at least 5 times without O-ring cutting or extrusion;
5. M4 bolt torque is finalized.

Design basis: Parker static face-seal guidance recommends relatively high squeeze for axial static seals; 25% is within the normal static-face design range.
