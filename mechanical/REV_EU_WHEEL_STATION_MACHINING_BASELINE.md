# PX-1 Rev.EU — machinable wheel station baseline

Status: prototype detail candidate based on the CRP150-type side-drive architecture already adopted for PX-1.

## Functional stack
Each of the six wheel stations is contained entirely in P1 or P2 and does not penetrate P0.

From inner side-bay side toward the wheel:
1. inner 61801 bearing support;
2. m1 Z50 wheel gear on Ø12 keyed journal;
3. second 61801 support;
4. stepped shaft shoulder Ø12 -> Ø17;
5. removable axle flange;
6. 61903 outer wheel-load bearing;
7. dynamic FKM sealing element on dedicated polished Ø17 journal;
8. dirt-exclusion labyrinth;
9. profiled Ø90-class wheel;
10. independent axial retaining washer/bolt.

## Shaft candidate PX1-431-6W
Material candidates:
- 40Х13 / AISI 420 hardened and polished, or
- precipitation-hardening stainless if supplier/machining route is better.

Nominal functional diameters:
- gear/bearing inner journal: Ø12 h6 candidate;
- outer wheel-support and seal journal: Ø17 h6 candidate;
- thread at external end: M6 internal axial thread preferred over reducing shaft section with external thread.

Torque transfer:
- 4x4 mm parallel key on Z50 journal and wheel hub where space allows;
- no D-flat or keyway under the dynamic sealing lip.

## Bearing locations
Use 61801 12x21x5 for compact inner supports and 61903 17x30x7 as the main outer wheel-load bearing.

Axial location should use machined shoulders + thin spacer/shim rather than relying on bearing clamp through the rotating seal.

## Dynamic seal
Exact seal type remains a procurement/bench gate. Preferred prototype order:
- FKM double-lip radial shaft seal if a compact 17 mm ID article fits the flange;
- X-ring/quad-ring only if friction/wear testing is acceptable;
- labyrinth outside the primary dynamic seal in all cases.

Seal-running surface target:
- no keyway/thread under lip;
- Ra <=0.4 µm target;
- lead-free polish/grind finish;
- entry chamfer 0.5x20° class;
- hardness adequate for wet abrasive duty.

## Removable flange
Current prototype flange:
- OD ~50 mm;
- aluminum or stainless depending final corrosion/wear test;
- carries 61903 and dynamic seal;
- local static FKM O-ring to side-cover/body interface;
- 3xM4 or 4xM4 fasteners outside local seal path;
- two small extraction/jacking holes desirable for service.

The flange must be removable without opening P0.

## Wheel retention
Wheel torque and wheel axial retention are separate functions.

Preferred:
- keyed/splined hub transmits torque;
- M6 screw + large retained washer/disc provides axial retention;
- screw uses threadlocker or mechanical locking compatible with repeated wet service;
- no clamping load transmitted through the dynamic seal lip.

## Prototype dimensions to freeze only after parts are in hand
- exact bearing internal clearance/seal suffix;
- exact dynamic seal OD/width;
- flange O-ring article and groove;
- wheel hub width and key length;
- shaft overall length;
- spacer/shim thicknesses.

## Acceptance tests
- bearing preload/endplay measurement;
- hand rotation torque dry and wet;
- 1 h submerged rotation at normal positive pressure;
- sand/mud contamination around labyrinth;
- 100 forward/reverse cycles;
- wheel-side radial load test;
- pressure decay isolated to P1/P2;
- post-test shaft seal track inspection.
