# PX-1 Rev.DX — integrated CRP150-style drivetrain packaging

Status: CAD packaging candidate validated numerically; not machining release.

## Integrated changes
This revision combines Rev.DT–DW into one current mechanical master:
- six wheels, three per side;
- five equal m1 Z50 side gears per side;
- KHK SB1.5-1845H / SB1.5-4518H 2.5:1 bevel input;
- separately supported Ø8 pinion shafts;
- JGB37-555-class Ø37 motors;
- one motor mounted toward the front and the other toward the rear so their Ø37 bodies do not require side-by-side width;
- common +0.20…+0.30 bar pressurized dry body/side-bay volume;
- scalloped side-cover concept instead of a simple rectangular slab;
- tapered Ø90-class wheel profile for DN150.

## Key drivetrain coordinates
Reference master:
- wheel centers X = 50 / 150 / 250 mm;
- idlers X = 100 / 200 mm;
- wheel axis Z = 45 mm;
- bevel center X = 150 mm, Z = 45 mm;
- left bevel intersection Y = +5 mm;
- right bevel intersection Y = -5 mm.

The small lateral offset is possible because the motors occupy different longitudinal zones.

## Side drive
Per side:
`Z50 wheel -> Z50 idler -> Z50 wheel/input -> Z50 idler -> Z50 wheel`

- gear pitch Ø50;
- OD Ø52;
- adjacent centers 50 mm;
- wheel pitch 100 mm;
- target face width 8 mm.

Starting production method is a KHK SSG1-50-class hardened/ground blank or equivalent, Ø12 bore, with non-tooth hub material reduced to the compact PX-1 axial envelope and a proper keyway added.

## Wheel shaft update
Preferred side-drive shaft family is now Ø12 mm.
Starting outer support:
- 61801-2RS, 12x21x5;
- rotary seal candidate 12x22x7 FKM;
- keyed wheel hub;
- M6 axial retainer.

The middle shaft uses a Ø10 bevel-gear seat transitioning to Ø12 after the large bevel.

## Side cover
The visual/structural concept now follows the logic seen in DRW-002-374 more closely:
- long cover follows five gear stations;
- scalloped/lobed upper/lower contour around the gears;
- broad cover plate nominal 5 mm;
- local wheel bearing/seal bosses protrude farther than the general plate;
- continuous O-ring remains mandatory;
- cover is not a gearbox cassette.

## DN150 wheel contact correction
The wheel is not supposed to have clearance everywhere: its tread must intentionally contact the pipe.

Current ideal contact geometry:
- narrow full-radius tread crown reaches approximately Y = 57 mm;
- that crown is the intended DN150 contact line;
- pipe axis for the ideal cross-section becomes Z ≈ 48.744 mm;
- outboard shoulder tapers strongly, ending around Y = 68.5 mm / radius ≈14.8 mm.

Calculated ideal clearances after that intentional tread contact:
- main body corner: ~13.2 mm;
- bevel belly envelope: ~18.6 mm;
- broad side-cover plate: ~13.8 mm;
- wheel boss: ~16.4 mm;
- tapered shoulder at Y60: ~2.4 mm;
- tapered shoulder at Y64: ~4.16 mm;
- outer wheel face Y68.5: ~4.03 mm.

Near the tread contact line clearance intentionally tends to zero.

## Camera DN150 recheck
The corrected vehicle support position slightly reduces previous camera margin.

For the current Ø52 x72 mm camera solid and full TILT -105…+105° ideal sweep:
- camera-axis Z75 -> ~4.34 mm nominal minimum clearance;
- camera-axis Z76 -> ~3.34 mm nominal minimum clearance.

Therefore **DN150-SAFE = Z75 mm candidate**. Z76 still passes an ideal 3 mm calculation but is not preferred because real lens rings, fasteners, cable loops, tire compliance and pipe tolerances will consume the margin.

## CAD result
A CadQuery packaging master is generated by `mechanical/cadquery/PX1_CRP150_6W_Master_RevDX.py`.

The check currently reports zero unintended solid intersection for:
- front vs rear motor envelopes;
- each motor vs its middle side gear;
- each large bevel envelope vs the adjacent Z50 side gear (0.5 mm nominal axial packaging gap in the simplified model).

Intended bevel tooth-mesh intersection is not used as a collision failure.
