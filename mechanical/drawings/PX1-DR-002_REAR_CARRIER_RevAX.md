# PX1-DR-002 — Rear bearing/seal carrier — Rev.AX

Status: DRAWING-CANDIDATE except static body-seal groove.

## Function
Removable rear pressure-boundary carrier for one crawler side. Supports the 6000-2RS bearing, 10x22x7 FKM rotary seal and the Ø10 rear output shaft.

## Main geometry
- overall length: 28.0 mm
- outer flange: Ø56 x 5 mm
- body locating spigot: Ø38 candidate, length 23 mm
- shaft through-clearance: Ø12
- bearing seat: Ø26 H7, depth 8.2 mm
- rotary-seal seat: Ø22 H8, depth 7.2 mm
- mounting: 4x Ø4.5 clearance holes on PCD 48, angular positions 45/135/225/315 deg

## Critical geometric requirements
1. Bearing seat and seal seat should be machined in one setup where practical.
2. Coaxiality/concentricity target between bearing and seal seats: <=0.03 mm.
3. Seal-seat entry requires a small lead-in/chamfer so the FKM outer diameter is not cut during installation.
4. No burrs on shaft through-bore or seal seat.
5. Carrier must remain removable from outside the dry body.

## Materials
Prototype preference: EN AW-6082 T6 with suitable hard-anodized/corrosion-protected finish. A stainless carrier remains an alternate if field corrosion or seal-seat wear makes aluminium unsuitable.

## Purchased interfaces
- 6000-2RS bearing: 10x26x8 mm
- FKM radial seal: 10x22x7 mm
- shaft: PX1-DR-001, Ø10 critical journals

## Static body seal — HOLD
The static seal between this carrier and the main pressure body is intentionally not released in Rev.AX. The exact O-ring cross-section, groove dimensions, squeeze and body mating land must be selected and verified before production machining.

Do NOT improvise the O-ring groove at the machine shop.

## First-article checks
- bearing press/transition fit with the actual 6000-2RS;
- seal installation with no outer-rubber damage;
- free rotation of assembled shaft;
- no contact at Ø12 through clearance;
- carrier removal with normal hand tools;
- pressure/leak test after static flange seal is finalized.

## Release gate
Rev.AX may be machined only as a first-article carrier if the static body seal area is deliberately left with extra stock or omitted per manufacturing instruction. Full RELEASE requires the finalized O-ring interface and body mating drawing.
