# PX1-DR-002 — Rear bearing/seal carrier — Rev.AZ

Status: DRAWING-CANDIDATE. First article only until assembled leak test passes.

## Function
Removable rear carrier supporting one output shaft, one 6000-2RS bearing, one 10x22x7 FKM rotary shaft seal, and one static 40x2 FKM face O-ring between carrier flange and pressure body.

## Material
Preferred prototype: EN AW-6082 T6 aluminium. 7075-T6 may be evaluated if required. If aluminium is used, protect sealing and fitting surfaces during any anodizing process.

## Main dimensions
- overall length: 28.0 mm
- cylindrical body OD: 42.0 mm
- mounting flange OD: 60.0 mm
- flange thickness: 5.0 mm
- centering pilot: Ø38 h7 candidate x 4.0 mm
- shaft through-clearance: Ø12 mm
- bearing seat: Ø26 H7 x 8.2 mm deep
- seal seat: Ø22 H8 x 7.2 mm deep
- mount holes: 4x Ø4.5 mm on PCD54, equally spaced, 45° clocking

## Static face seal
O-ring: 40x2 mm FKM, 75–80 Shore A target.
Groove candidate:
- groove ID: 39.0 mm
- groove OD: 44.0 mm
- radial width: 2.50 mm
- groove depth: 1.50 mm
- nominal squeeze: 25%

Do not release the groove until the actual O-ring cross-section is checked with calipers and first-article compression is verified.

## Critical geometry
1. Bearing-seat axis and rotary-seal-seat axis concentricity: <=0.03 mm target.
2. Mounting face runout relative to bearing axis: <=0.03 mm target.
3. Pilot OD and bearing/seal bores should be machined from common datum/setup where practical.
4. O-ring groove bottom and mounting face must be free of radial scratches crossing the seal path.
5. Seal seat lead-in must not cut or roll the FKM seal OD during assembly.

## Purchased interfaces
- bearing: 6000-2RS, 10x26x8 mm
- rotary shaft seal: 10x22x7 FKM
- static O-ring: 40x2 FKM
- mounting screws: M4 stainless, final length and head style pending pressure-body wall definition

## First-article inspection
Record:
- flange OD and thickness;
- pilot OD;
- Ø26 H7 bearing bore;
- Ø22 H8 seal bore;
- PCD54 hole positions;
- O-ring groove ID/OD/depth;
- bore concentricity/runout where inspection equipment permits.

## Assembly/leak validation
Carrier + actual O-ring + actual rotary seal + PX1-DR-001 shaft must be assembled before release. Pressure test the dry body, rotate the shaft during part of the test, and inspect for pressure decay and visible moisture. Final acceptance pressure/duration remain controlled by the system-level leak-test procedure.
