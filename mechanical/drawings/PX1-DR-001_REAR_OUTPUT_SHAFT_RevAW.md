# PX1-DR-001 — Rear output shaft — Rev.AW

Status: DRAWING-CANDIDATE. Do not manufacture production quantity yet; first article only.

## Function
Rear pressure-boundary output shaft for one crawler side. Carries external drivetrain/wheel while running in 6000-2RS bearing and 10x22x7 FKM rotary seal.

## Material
Preferred prototype: AISI 316L stainless steel. Alternate martensitic stainless may be evaluated only after corrosion, hardness and seal-wear review.

## Main dimensions
- overall length: 64.0 mm
- main shaft diameter: Ø10 mm
- bearing journal: Ø10 h6, length 8 mm
- rotary-seal journal: Ø10 h6; continuous smooth land, minimum 12 mm axial provision
- external keyed zone: Ø10; keyway 3x3 mm only outside seal-running land
- threaded end: M8x1.25-6g, thread length 14 mm
- thread lead-in: 0.5x45° target
- unspecified edge break: 0.2–0.5x45°

## Critical surface requirements
1. Seal-running land: Ra 0.2–0.4 µm target.
2. No axial scratches, thread, keyway or sharp transition under seal lip.
3. Bearing and seal journals to be made in one setup where practical.
4. TIR of seal journal relative to bearing journal: <=0.02 mm target.
5. Protect seal land from chuck/jaw damage after finish operation.

## Fits / purchased interfaces
- bearing: 6000-2RS, 10x26x8 mm
- seal: 10x22x7 FKM spring-loaded radial shaft seal
- retention: M8x1.25 DIN 982 A2 self-locking nut, 13 mm wrench target
- washer: M8 DIN 125 A / ISO 7089, 8.4x16x1.6 mm

## General tolerance
ISO 2768-mK candidate for non-critical dimensions. Explicit fit/surface requirements override general tolerance.

## First-article inspection
Measure and record:
- overall length;
- bearing-journal diameter at 3 axial positions, two directions each;
- seal-journal diameter at 3 axial positions, two directions each;
- TIR between bearing and seal journals;
- surface finish of seal land if tester is available;
- M8 thread go/no-go or verified mating nut;
- key fit after keyway is finalized.

## Release hold
This drawing becomes RELEASE only after the first machined shaft is assembled with the actual bearing, actual FKM seal, wheel/hub and selected M8 hardware, then passes rotation and leak testing.
