## Standard hardware baseline
- side covers: approximately 12–16 M4 A4 stainless fasteners per side after final contour;
- axle flanges: 3 fasteners each, M3/M4 candidate;
- top cover: approximately 10–14 M4 A4;
- motor holder: 4 main clamp screws + 2 locating dowels;
- motor adapters: vendor-specific M3/M4 hardware;
- wheel retainers: 6x M6 stainless plus suitable locking washer system;
- circlips/retaining rings for wheel and pinion shaft axial control;
- 4x4 keys for wheel gears/wheels where geometry allows.

## Service spares for first field kit
Minimum first kit:
- 4x wheel dynamic seals;
- 4x axle-flange O-rings;
- 2x side-cover O-rings;
- 2x 61801;
- 1x 61903;
- 1x 61800;
- 1x Z50 gear;
- 1x idler bushing/bearing;
- 2x wheel retaining screws/locking washers;
- seal-compatible grease and anti-galling compound.

## Do not bulk-order yet
Do not buy production quantity of motors, KHK gears, dynamic seals, molded side-cover O-rings or tires until one complete left-side drivetrain mock-up has passed fit, pressure and loaded endurance testing.
