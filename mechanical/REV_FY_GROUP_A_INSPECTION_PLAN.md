# PX-1 Rev.FY — Group A prototype inspection plan

Status: prototype quality-control plan tied to Rev.FX drawing candidates.

## General rule
Do not accept parts by visual fit alone. Every rotating/sealing part receives a dimensional record before assembly.

## DRW-PX1-431 wheel shaft
Measure:
- Ø12 bearing/gear journal with 0.001 mm micrometer;
- Ø17 61903 journal;
- Ø19 dynamic seal land;
- wheel-seat Ø17;
- total length;
- axial segment lengths;
- runout of Ø19 seal land relative to Ø17 bearing journal.

Surface checks:
- seal land Ra <=0.4 um target;
- no spiral turning marks under X-ring;
- no burr at keyway or M6 end near seal path.

Acceptance: quarantine any shaft with visible seal-land damage even if diameter is in tolerance.

## DRW-PX1-432 axle flange
Measure:
- Ø30 61903 pocket at 0/90 deg and two depths;
- flange locating pilot;
- face flatness;
- PCD40 hole positions;
- coaxiality of pocket to pilot.

Trial-fit one actual 61903 before producing all six flanges.

## DRW-PX1-433 side cover
Measure on surface plate/CMM where possible:
- overall 286 x 86 x 5;
- three pilot centers relative to cover datum;
- 12 screw-hole positions;
- sealing-face flatness;
- seal-groove width/depth at minimum 8 points once groove is released.

Leak coupon gate:
- machine the selected O-ring groove first in a small coupon;
- 20 open/close cycles;
- +0.25 bar dry decay and submerged bubble check;
- only then release both full side covers.

## DRW-PX1-434 X200 output shaft
Measure:
- Ø10 gear seat;
- Ø10 61800 journal;
- Ø18 seal land;
- Ø12 coupling seat;
- total length 42;
- runout bearing-to-seal land <=0.02 mm target.

Seal land receives the same no-spiral-mark rule as wheel shafts.

## DRW-PX1-435 bearing/seal boss
Measure:
- Ø19 bearing pocket;
- Ø30 seal pocket;
- bore coaxiality <=0.03 mm target;
- face perpendicularity to bore axis.

Trial assembly with actual 61800 and exact 18x30x7 seal before body machining is frozen.

## DRW-PX1-436 pinion shaft
Measure:
- Ø6 motor-coupling seat;
- Ø8 bevel seat;
- Ø12 61801 journal;
- shoulder locations;
- total length 39.

Trial-fit actual small bevel gear and 61801. Final axial-retention geometry is frozen only after this trial.

## Assembly-level checks after Group A machining
1. Assemble one dry side drive without seals and verify hand rotation.
2. Measure gear backlash at all five Z50 meshes.
3. Install seals and record breakaway torque.
4. Pressurize side bay to +0.25 bar and rotate all three wheel shafts.
5. Run 30 min at prototype wheel speed.
6. Inspect X-ring lands for polishing, scoring or heat.
7. Repeat leak test.

## Documentation
Record actual values by serial number for each shaft/flange/boss. The first prototype becomes the tolerance-learning lot; do not silently widen drawing tolerances to pass bad parts.
