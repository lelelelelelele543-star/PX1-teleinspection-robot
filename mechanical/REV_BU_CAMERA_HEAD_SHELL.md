# PX-1 Rev.BU — camera-head shell, window and quick release

Status: DRAWING-CANDIDATE packaging; not production release.

## Shell
- outside diameter: 52 mm target;
- length: 78 mm target;
- aluminium shell: EN AW-6082 T6 candidate;
- nominal wall: 2.5 mm;
- front structural ring reserved: 8 mm;
- rear quick-release zone reserved: 10 mm.

## Optical front
- window candidate: Ø28 x 3 mm;
- material: tempered mineral glass or sapphire candidate;
- window is statically sealed; exact O-ring and groove remain HOLD until exact window dimensions/tolerances are selected;
- LED illumination is placed as an annulus around the optical window.

## Quick release
Field-service target:
1. unplug electrical interface;
2. release one retained latch;
3. withdraw camera head axially;
4. no loose screws/parts required for normal head removal.

Mechanical concept:
- Ø36 mm-class rear spigot envelope;
- one anti-rotation key;
- one retained radial latch;
- axial static O-ring between head spigot and lift cradle.

The latch must carry retention only; electrical connector must not carry bending or impact load.

## ROLL sealing blocker
Continuous ROLL still requires a real rotary waterproof boundary. Bearings alone are not a seal. Final shell drawing is blocked until we freeze one of these architectures:
- radial shaft seal on a dedicated polished roll sleeve;
- compact mechanical-face seal;
- fully sealed internal rotating cartridge with static outer shell.

Preference for PX-1: sealed internal rotating cartridge, because the outer Ø52 shell can remain static and easier to seal/repair.

## Next gate
Build the static-outer-shell / internal-roll-cartridge concept in CAD, then fit the two 6803 bearings, rotary video transfer and ROLL gear inside it. If it fits, this becomes the preferred waterproof ROLL architecture and DN150 is re-run with real front and rear external geometry.
