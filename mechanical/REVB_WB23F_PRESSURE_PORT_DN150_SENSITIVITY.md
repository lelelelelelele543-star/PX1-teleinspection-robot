# PX-1 Rev.B WB23F — pressure-port DN150 sensitivity

Date: 2026-09-14
Status: **PACKAGING RULE FROZEN / VALVE ARTICLE PROCUREMENT HOLD**

## Purpose

The pressure/fill valve is the limiting fixed item on the current WB23E top service cover when the crawler is in LOW and must pass an ideal DN150 cylinder.

WB23F therefore controls the valve procurement envelope before an exact article is selected.

## Current position

- service-cover baseline: WB23E, 86 x 44 x 6 mm;
- nominal pressure-port centre: X273 / Y0;
- only LOW must fit DN150;
- current conservative reference envelope: Ø14 x 6 mm exposed above the cover;
- that envelope leaves about 4.70 mm ideal-DN150 clearance.

## Sensitivity screen

Approximate ideal-DN150 clearance at the current port centre:

| Exposed hard envelope | Ideal DN150 clearance |
|---|---:|
| Ø10 x 5 mm | ~5.87 mm |
| Ø12 x 5 mm | ~5.79 mm |
| Ø14 x 5 mm | ~5.69 mm |
| Ø12 x 6 mm | ~4.79 mm |
| Ø14 x 6 mm | ~4.70 mm |
| Ø14 x 7 mm | ~3.70 mm |
| Ø14 x 7.5 mm | ~3.21 mm |
| Ø14 x 8 mm | ~2.71 mm — FAIL current 3 mm screen |

## Procurement rule

Preferred valve/cap exposed envelope:

**<= Ø12 x 5 mm** above the service-cover outer face.

Acceptable prototype envelope after exact CAD re-check:

**<= Ø14 x 6 mm**.

Do not buy a valve/cap combination taller than about 7.5 mm as a baseline part. Ø14 x 8 mm already fails the current 3 mm ideal-DN150 packaging screen at the present position.

The protective cap counts in the hard envelope. A small valve with a tall cap is not acceptable merely because the bare valve body fits.

## Preferred construction

Prefer, in order:

1. recessed or semi-recessed fill valve with a compact removable cap;
2. low-profile threaded service valve contained largely inside the 6 mm cover/boss geometry;
3. only if necessary, move/reprofile the local service-cover boss after a full WB25 integration check.

Do not enlarge the whole crawler body to accommodate a convenient valve.

## Release gates

Before freezing the pressure-port machining drawing:

- obtain 2–3 real valve candidates;
- measure body OD, exposed height, cap OD/height and required tool clearance;
- verify thread and sealing method;
- update the CAD with the actual solid envelope;
- perform physical DN150 jig check;
- perform +0.25 bar pressure-decay and submerged leak test;
- repeat after valve open/close/service cycles.
