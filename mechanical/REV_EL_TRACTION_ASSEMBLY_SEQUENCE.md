# PX-1 Rev.EL — traction assembly / service sequence

Status: prototype build procedure skeleton. Final torques and lubricants are HOLD until actual fasteners/seals are selected.

## A. Main housing preparation
1. Clean all P0, P1 and P2 machined cavities and oil passages/pressure ports.
2. Verify bearing bores, shaft-seal bores and side-cover lands are burr-free.
3. Measure/coaxially inspect left/right central transfer bores.
4. Install P0-side 61800 bearings and transfer-shaft seal carriers.
5. Lubricate FKM lips with compatible assembly lubricant only.
6. Install left/right central transfer shafts without large bevel gears first and verify free rotation.

## B. Central bevel output
1. Fit actual KHK large bevel gear to each transfer shaft using the released positive torque connection.
2. Install axial retaining ring/shoulder hardware.
3. Set transfer-shaft axial float with defined shims.
4. Confirm the Ø18 seal journal sits fully under the 18x30x7 lip with no keyway/thread crossing the lip.
5. Pressure-test P0 with side bays vented before installing the motors.

## C. Paired motor holder
1. Assemble each stepped pinion shaft with its 61801 and KHK Z18 gear.
2. Install the two pinion shafts into the common motor holder.
3. Mount actual JGB37-555 motors through replaceable adapters.
4. Fit split-clamp motor-to-pinion couplings.
5. Bench-run both motors outside the crawler at low current and check runout/noise.
6. Install complete paired holder into P0 using dowels and defined shim pack.
7. Mark bevel teeth, hand-rotate, inspect contact pattern.
8. Only after correct contact apply final holder clamp torque.

## D. Side drive subassembly, each side
1. Install four idler pins/bushings as applicable for the side being built.
2. Preassemble front and rear short axles with keys and Z50 gears.
3. Preassemble middle long axle with Z50 and service-coupling half.
4. Install 61801 bearings in the side-drive structural seats.
5. Insert three wheel axles and set axial retention.
6. Install two Z50 idlers.
7. Hand-turn the five-gear train through at least 20 revolutions.
8. Verify no tight point with the side cover temporarily torqued.
9. Engage the middle long axle to the central transfer-shaft dog/keyed coupling.

## E. Axle flanges and wheel seals
For each of six wheel stations:
1. Fit 61903 into removable axle flange.
2. Fit selected dynamic FKM seal/quad-ring in correct orientation.
3. Lightly lubricate seal lip and shaft entry chamfer.
4. Fit flange static O-ring.
5. Slide flange over the Ø17 outer journal without rolling/cutting the lip.
6. Tighten three flange screws evenly.
7. Verify wheel axle still rotates freely by hand.

## F. Main side cover
1. Clean main P1/P2 O-ring groove and cover sealing face.
2. Fit side-cover O-ring dry or with only approved assembly grease.
3. Locate cover by pilot/dowels.
4. Tighten perimeter screws in alternating sequence in 2–3 passes.
5. Do not use screws to pull a misaligned cover into position.
6. Pressure-test that side zone alone to +0.25 bar and record 30–60 min decay/temperature.

## G. Wheels
1. Install keyed wheel hub/traction ring.
2. Fit labyrinth/dirt shield geometry.
3. Install retaining disk and M6 locking hardware.
4. Verify retaining bolt does not transmit drive torque.
5. Spin all six wheels by hand before power is applied.

## H. Powered drivetrain validation
1. Current-limited power supply, wheels off ground.
2. Run left side forward/reverse at low voltage/PWM.
3. Run right side forward/reverse.
4. Verify all three wheels on each side rotate same direction and similar speed.
5. Log motor current and 24 V bus behavior.
6. Increase to 24 V only after mechanical check passes.
7. Run 100 reversing cycles and inspect temperature/noise.

## I. Pressure + submerged test
1. Fill P0/P1/P2 separately to nominal +0.25 bar.
2. Confirm one vented side does not depressurize the other two zones.
3. Static submersion.
4. Powered wheel rotation submerged.
5. Recheck pressure decay after rotation.
6. Inspect each wheel flange for water tracking past the dynamic seal.

## J. Service philosophy
Normal wheel-seal service must not require opening P0.
Normal gear service opens only the affected P1/P2 side cover.
Motor service opens P0 top cover and removes the paired holder while large bevel/output shafts stay installed.

No threadlocker, grease, anti-seize or tightening torque is frozen until exact material pairs and seal compatibility are released.
