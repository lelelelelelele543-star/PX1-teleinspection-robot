# PX-1 Rev.CT — integrated 4WD geartrain, no cassette

Status: architecture update. Supersedes the removable side gearbox cassette concept from Rev.CS.

## User requirement
No separate gearbox cassettes.

## Architecture
Each side of the crawler uses an integrated gear train mounted directly to the crawler structural side wall:
- one JGB37-520 24 V motor per side;
- motor is inside the dry pressure body;
- front and rear wheel shafts are supported directly by bearings in the main body/side bearing carriers;
- external spur gears couple the two wheel shafts;
- gears sit in the wet/dirty zone but are protected by a simple removable flat guard/cover;
- the guard is NOT a sealed gearbox and is NOT a structural cassette;
- no belt, chain or separate gearbox module.

## Service concept
Field service must be possible with basic hand tools:
1. remove wheel retaining fastener;
2. remove wheel if required;
3. undo simple gear guard screws;
4. inspect/replace exposed spur gear or idler;
5. replace shaft seal/bearing from the side without dismantling the electronics stack where practical.

The main pressure body is not opened for routine cleaning of the wheel gears.

## Contamination philosophy
Do not attempt to make the wheel-to-wheel gear train oil-tight. Sewer grit will eventually reach an external transmission. Prefer:
- coarse, strong metal gears;
- generous backlash;
- drainage openings at the lowest point of the guard;
- flushing access;
- no grease pocket that traps sand into abrasive paste;
- guard geometry that blocks stones/rags while allowing water and fine dirt to leave.

## Gear candidate
Keep Rev.CS starting geometry only as a sizing candidate:
- module 1.0;
- 20 degree pressure angle;
- 8–10 mm face width;
- wheel gears around z40;
- steel preferred for final wheel gears/idlers.

Exact tooth counts depend on frozen wheelbase and motor placement.

## Motor drive
The JGB37 motor must not carry wheel radial load. Motor pinion drives a supported wheel gear/idler. Wheel shafts have their own bearings and seals.

## Sealing
Each wheel shaft crosses the dry pressure-body boundary through its own replaceable sealing arrangement. Seal selection and shaft finish are RELEASE gates. The external gears remain outside that seal boundary.

## Release gates
- frozen wheelbase and body width;
- exact wheel shaft diameter;
- shaft bearing selection;
- replaceable shaft seal arrangement;
- exact gear tooth counts and center distances;
- guard clearance with Ø90 wheel;
- mud/sand flushing test;
- loaded drive and jam test.
