# PX-1 Rev.DZ — removable axle flange solid candidate

Status: prototype machining candidate; dimensional freeze after seal/bearing samples.

## Function
Each of the six wheel exits gets its own small removable axle flange. This follows the successful service philosophy visible in the CRP150 side-drive drawings but uses PX-1 dimensions and common parts.

The flange is NOT a gearbox cassette. The side gears remain mounted to the crawler structure. The flange only carries the outer bearing, primary shaft seal and dirt-exclusion geometry.

## Baseline geometry
- flange outside diameter: Ø48 mm;
- flange material: Al 6082-T6, hard anodized or suitable corrosion treatment;
- locating pilot into side cover/body: Ø36 h7 candidate;
- flange thickness at mounting land: 6 mm;
- external seal/bearing boss kept inside the tapered wheel hub recess;
- three M4 fasteners on PCD40 preferred over the earlier M3 concept for repeated service;
- two small extraction/jacking holes optional, not connected to the pressure cavity.

## Static flange seal
Candidate:
- FKM O-ring 30 x 1.5 mm;
- face seal groove machined in flange or parent wall, not split between both parts;
- initial squeeze target ~20–25%;
- no bolt hole may intersect the O-ring groove;
- pilot locates the flange; screws provide clamp only.

Exact groove dimensions follow the actual O-ring supplier standard and measured finish.

## Outer bearing + seal stack
From pressurized P1/P2 toward sewer:
1. 61801-2RS bearing, 12 x 21 x 5 mm;
2. spacer/shoulder;
3. FKM TC rotary seal 12 x 22 x 7 mm;
4. grease-free or very lightly greased dirt labyrinth;
5. wheel recess/hub.

The bearing and seal must not share one uncontrolled counterbore. Each receives a defined axial shoulder.

Candidate fits:
- bearing housing: Ø21 H7;
- seal housing: Ø22 H8;
- shaft bearing journal: Ø12 h6;
- seal journal: Ø12 h8 or better, Ra <=0.4 um target;
- bearing shoulder perpendicularity/runout <=0.03 mm relative to shaft axis target.

## Seal protection
Primary seal lip must never run over:
- keyway;
- thread;
- retaining-ring groove;
- cross-hole;
- sharp shaft step.

Provide:
- entry lead-in approximately 0.5 x 20 deg;
- polished uninterrupted journal;
- external labyrinth with 0.3–0.5 mm radial clearance candidate;
- replaceable thin dirt washer/shield if field tests show grit packing.

## Wheel interface
Wheel torque is transmitted positively by the shaft/hub interface, not by the M6 end screw.

Baseline:
- Ø12 shaft;
- 4x4 parallel key candidate for wheel/Z50 interfaces where hub thickness permits;
- M6 axial retaining screw + retaining disk + Schnorr/spring washer;
- optional O-ring under retaining disk only as contamination exclusion, not pressure boundary.

## Service rule
A technician must be able to replace the outer bearing and shaft seal without removing electronics or the opposite side drive.

Procedure target:
1. depressurize affected side zone only;
2. remove wheel;
3. remove three flange screws;
4. pull flange with bearing/seal as one service piece;
5. inspect shaft journal;
6. press new bearing/seal into flange with simple mandrels;
7. install new static O-ring;
8. torque, re-pressure and leak-test the affected zone.

## Release gates
- confirm actual 61801 OD/width and selected FKM seal dimensions;
- verify PCD40 tool access with wheel removed;
- pressure test one flange blank at >=1 bar differential target before crawler assembly;
- 5000-revolution submerged shaft test;
- check seal lip temperature and torque drag;
- freeze drawing after successful service-cycle test.
