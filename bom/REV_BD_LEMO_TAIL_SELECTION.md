# PX-1 Rev.BD — LEMO tail selection freeze

## Body-side receptacle
Selected baseline: **LEMO EGG.5K.870.CLL5**.
Manufacturer data:
- K outdoor keyed series;
- fixed front-mounted receptacle, nut fixing;
- push-pull;
- IP68;
- insert 5K.870 = 2 coax 75 ohm + 10 low-voltage contacts;
- rated current: 8 A per low-voltage contact under manufacturer conditions;
- panel thread / drill requirement: M45x1.5;
- dimensions: A=55 mm, L=47.5 mm, N=43.6 mm, S1=42.5 mm;
- endurance: 5000 mating cycles.

## Cable-side plug family
Matching straight plug family: **FGG.5K.870.CLA...**.
The final suffix depends on actual tether jacket outside diameter.

Verified manufacturer example:
- FGG.5K.870.CLAC105: cable OD 9.60–10.50 mm, A=38 mm, L=92 mm;
- manufacturer also lists FGG.5K.870.CLAC155 for cable OD 14.60–15.50 mm as a suggested match on the receptacle page.

## HOLD before purchase
Measure the actual cable OD at 5 points over at least 1 m of the intended tether, including over any ovality. Select the collet/suffix only after the measured OD range is known. Do not force an incorrect collet size.

## Electrical note
The 5K.870 insert is especially suitable for PX-1 because it provides two 75-ohm coax contacts plus ten low-voltage contacts. Final current allocation and parallel-contact use for 24 V power remain subject to cable conductor cross-section, connector derating and full-system voltage-drop calculation.

## Mechanical note
Connector shell is not a towing eye. Tether pulling force must be reacted through an independent mechanical strain-relief/tow path.
