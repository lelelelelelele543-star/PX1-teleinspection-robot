# PX-1 Rev.AT — rear seal and wheel-retention procurement

## Rotary shaft seal — SELECTED SIZE / SOURCE CANDIDATES
Required geometry remains 10 x 22 x 7 mm.
Preferred material: FKM.

Verified current market candidate:
- DICHTOMATIK / Freudenberg FST, WA NIF 10.0 x 22.0 x 7.0, material FP803403, 80 FKM.
- Rubber-covered OD, spring-loaded radial shaft seal, no auxiliary dust lip in this WA configuration.
- Supplier lists 0.5 bar pressure capability, -25..+150 C and shaft speed capability far above PX-1 requirements.

Russian-market candidate:
- reinforced FKM type 2, 10 x 22 x 7 is listed by TNMK.
- Because marketplace/catalog descriptions can contain errors, this candidate is PURCHASE-INSPECTION REQUIRED: verify actual marking, dimensions, spring and lip direction before installation.

Decision: retain CAD seal seat Ø22 H8 and shaft journal Ø10 h6. Do not enlarge to 25 mm unless the 10x22x7 supply becomes impractical.

## Seal orientation
Primary spring-loaded lip faces the wet/pressure side. Grease cavity/labyrinth remains outside the dry electronics volume. Final orientation drawing follows physical seal inspection.

## Wheel retention
Prototype remains M8 external retention.
Preferred hardware direction:
- stainless A2/A4 flat washer to ISO 7089 / DIN 125A geometry;
- stainless self-locking nut or all-metal prevailing-torque nut.

Do NOT use nylon/polyamide DIN125 washers found in electronics catalogs for wheel retention.

## Tool envelope
Maintain Ø20 mm minimum clear socket envelope until the exact M8 nut across-flats and socket OD are frozen.
