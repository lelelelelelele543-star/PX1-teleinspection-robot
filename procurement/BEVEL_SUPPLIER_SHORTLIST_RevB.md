## A. Czech custom-manufacturing candidates

### ZPA PRAGA, a.s.
Website: https://www.zpa-praga.cz/

Publicly stated capability:
- design and manufacture of bevel gears;
- straight teeth;
- curved Gleason and Oerlikon teeth;
- complete gear sets;
- small, medium and large series.

Why shortlisted:
- directly relevant bevel-gear specialization;
- manufacturer states both design and production capability;
- no public minimum module found on the reviewed page, so m1 feasibility must be asked explicitly.

RFQ priority: HIGH.

### Metalpreciz
Website: https://www.metalpreciz.cz/ozubena-kola/kuzelova-ozubena-kola

Publicly stated capability:
- custom bevel gears from customer drawings;
- straight and spiral bevel gears;
- piece and serial production.

Why shortlisted:
- explicitly advertises made-to-drawing bevel gears;
- suitable for a three-pair prototype inquiry.

RFQ priority: HIGH.

### ELITEX OK s.r.o.
Website: https://www.ozubenakola.eu/sortiment/

Publicly stated capability:
- custom gears and pinions;
- gears on cylindrical, conical and spherical surfaces;
- heat and thermo-chemical processing including hardening, carburizing, nitriding and high-frequency hardening;
- gear grinding on selected geometry.

Why shortlisted:
- unusually good match to the PX-1 need for custom geometry plus controlled hardening.

RFQ priority: HIGH.

### Strojírny Kalinowski
Website: https://strojirny.kalinowski.cz/cz/nase-produkce/vyroba-ozubeni.html

Publicly stated capability:
- manufacture of gearing;
- custom atypical bevel gearing by agreement;
- broad CNC/conventional gear-manufacturing capability.

Limit:
- public page does not clearly confirm m1 straight bevel capability or the required heat-treatment route.

RFQ priority: MEDIUM.

### TOS ZNOJMO
Website: https://d2.tos-znojmo.cz/cze/produkce/strojirenska-vyroba/kuzelove-soukoli/

Publicly stated capability:
- design, calculation, manufacture and measurement of straight and curved bevel sets;
- Gleason/Oerlikon capability.

Important limitation:
- published straight/curved bevel range starts at module 2 mm.

Decision:
- NOT a first-choice supplier for the exact m1 PX-1 pair;
- useful only if they can offer a special process outside the published range or advise a partner.

## C. RFQ order

Send the same controlled RFQ package first to:
1. ZPA PRAGA;
2. Metalpreciz;
3. ELITEX OK.

Then compare:
- confirmed m1 capability;
- tooth system;
- material;
- heat treatment;
- quoted continuous/transient pinion torque;
- mounting-distance/backlash control;
- matched-pair inspection;
- prototype quantity price;
- lead time.

Do not choose the cheapest quotation until the supplier explicitly confirms the torque and heat-treatment requirements.
