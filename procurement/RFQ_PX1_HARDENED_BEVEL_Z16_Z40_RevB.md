# RFQ — PX-1 hardened compact bevel gear pair

Project: PX-1 teleinspection crawler
Revision: Rev.B / WB03
Date: 2026-09-11
Quantity for prototype: 3 matched pairs preferred (2 crawler + 1 spare/test pair)

## Purpose

Request quotation, manufacturability review and controlled drawing for one matched compact bevel pair used in a wet-pipe inspection crawler. The pair replaces a proprietary CRP-150-class Z16/Z40 drive while preserving its compact envelope and service architecture.

This RFQ is deliberately functional. The manufacturer may recommend geometry corrections required by the selected Gleason/straight-bevel system, but the ratio and packaging envelope must not be changed without approval.

## Required kinematics

- shaft angle: 90 degrees;
- ratio: 2.5:1;
- nominal teeth: pinion Z16, gear Z40;
- nominal module: m = 1.0;
- straight bevel preferred;
- pressure angle target: 20 degrees;
- matched pair supplied together;
- intended rotation: reversible;
- low speed, high torque, intermittent crawler traction duty.

## Packaging envelope

Reconstructed source geometry target:
- pinion pitch diameter: approximately 16 mm;
- gear pitch diameter: approximately 40 mm;
- pinion OD target: approximately 18 mm class;
- large gear maximum OD target: <= 41 mm class unless a controlled drawing proves that a small increase remains packageable;
- nominal face width target: approximately 6.5 mm minimum;
- compact hub geometry required.

The manufacturer shall state:
- exact outside diameters;
- mounting distances;
- pitch-cone angles;
- cone distance;
- face width;
- recommended backlash;
- tooth quality/grade;
- required axial/radial location tolerances.

## Torque requirement

Normal crawler operation:
- expected pinion torque around 0.60 N.m for the present 50 N total pull target.

Minimum prototype acceptance requested:
- continuous allowable pinion torque >= 1.0 N.m;
- short-duration pinion overload >= 2.0 N.m.

Preferred final robustness target:
- continuous pinion capability >= 1.8 N.m if achievable in the required envelope;
- short transient capability toward 4.0 N.m if achievable without materially enlarging the pair.

Please state the allowable torque basis, duty assumptions and safety factors used.

## Material / heat treatment

Preferred manufacturer proposal, in priority order:
1. 42CrMo4 / 1.7225 with controlled tooth-flank induction hardening;
2. 16MnCr5 or SCM415-class carburized/case-hardened pair;
3. another gear steel with equal or better verified fatigue and wear performance.

Unhardened free-machining steel is not acceptable for the released traction pair.

Please state:
- material standard;
- core hardness;
- tooth-flank hardness;
- effective hardened depth;
- heat-treatment process;
- whether bores/hubs remain machinable after treatment;
- expected distortion and finish sequence.

## Pinion interface

Current architecture uses a separately supported bevel-pinion shaft adjacent to a Ø32 planetary motor.

Initial interface intent:
- pinion is mounted on its own shaft, not cantilevered directly on the motor gearbox output;
- shaft support uses 61801-2RS class bearing, 12 x 21 x 5 mm;
- final bore/key/spline geometry may be proposed by gear manufacturer after strength review;
- current motor output is 6 mm class and will couple to the supported pinion shaft through a separate serviceable coupling.

Do not assume the pinion bore must equal the motor shaft diameter.

## Large gear interface

Current architecture:
- large bevel gear on a transverse shaft;
- inboard support uses 61800-2RS class bearing, 10 x 19 x 5 mm;
- shaft crosses the dry-body bulkhead through an 18 x 30 x 7 dynamic shaft seal;
- output connects to the rear long-axle side-drive path.

Preferred final bore/retention shall be proposed after the manufacturer reviews the shaft load and hub wall thickness.

## Environment

- internal gear area is dry and positively pressurized during operation;
- external crawler works in sewer/wet inspection environment;
- gears will be grease lubricated;
- reversible operation;
- frequent start/stop;
- occasional stall/skid-turn transient;
- serviceability and replacement are required.

4. backlash range;
5. mounting-distance tolerance;
6. bore/hub machining state at delivery;

## Manufacturer questions

Please specifically answer:
- Can m1 Z16/Z40 straight bevel geometry at ~41 mm maximum large-gear OD meet >=1.0 N.m continuous pinion torque after suitable heat treatment?
- If not, what is the smallest dimensional change required while retaining 2.5:1 ratio?
- Can face width be increased modestly without increasing large-gear OD?
- Which material/heat-treatment route gives the best pitting resistance for low-speed reversible duty?
- What shaft/hub retention method is recommended at this size?

## Internal PX-1 release note

No supplier recommendation is automatically accepted. Any dimensional change must be checked against:
- CRP-150-derived body geometry;
- 18x30x7 large-shaft seal path;
- 61800/61801 bearing supports;
- motor holder;
- side-drive input alignment;
- folded camera/lift envelope;
- complete DN150 hard-part clearance.
