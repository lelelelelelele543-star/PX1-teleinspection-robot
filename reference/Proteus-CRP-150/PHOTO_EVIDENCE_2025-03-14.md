## Confirmed qualitative conclusions

1. The CRP-150 service philosophy is based on a large accessible crawler body rather than a stack of removable sealed cartridges.
2. The camera/lift assembly is mechanically distinct from the crawler dry electronics volume.
3. The lift linkage does not need to terminate directly on the camera's moving TILT shell; a fixed support/carrier exists between linkage and camera assembly.
4. The forward lift region is intentionally open to contamination/water and therefore must drain and remain serviceable.
5. Cable/connector routing in the wet region requires explicit strain relief and dirt protection; exposed service wiring seen during repair is not copied as a PX-1 production routing solution.
